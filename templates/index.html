Top view, same level. Click READY when you are set; the game starts when everyone in the room is ready. Each round: click where you want to stand, then move your mouse to point your laser and press Enter to fire. Nobody sees where anyone stands or aims until everyone has fired. Lasers stop where you aim. Get shot and you are out. If nobody gets hit, the room shrinks!
